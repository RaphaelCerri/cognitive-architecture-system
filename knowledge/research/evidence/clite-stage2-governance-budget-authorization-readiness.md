# C-lite Stage 2 Governance, Budget and Authorization Readiness

## Natureza

Governance proposal only. It does not authorize Stage 2, choose a prototype tool, assign real people, approve spending, recruit participants or create any visual artifact.

## Minimal future artifact package

| Artifact candidate | Included purpose | Excluded expansion |
|---|---|---|
| versioned scenario/content pack | synthetic repair-shop context, task seeds, state/evidence fixtures | executable/full dataset or final product copy |
| structured interactive prototype | eight surface families and declared variants/flows | frontend behavior, high-fidelity brand or complete platform |
| annotation pack | flow/state/authority/accessibility/content/simulation boundaries | separate Design System, token or architecture specification |
| evaluation package | approved protocol/script, observation/evidence records and findings/change log | universal score, certification or production evidence |

Existing Stage 1 maps/registers are inputs, not recreated visual artifacts. Four future artifacts are the ceiling unless Council amends scope.

## Tool capability requirements

Linked surface/state variants; deterministic transitions and reset/seed; keyboard-representable sequence; annotations; versioning/comments/change history; controlled sharing/access; export/archive; accessible interaction/viewing considerations; ability to identify simulation and prototype version. No product/vendor/library is researched or selected; capability gaps may change fidelity or stop Stage 2.

## Governance roles

| Role candidate | Responsibility | Authority boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Stage 2 owner | scope/version/budget coordination | cannot self-approve result |
| requirement owner | CL/PQ trace and change impact | cannot waive critical requirement alone |
| prototype creator | produce authorized artifacts | not evaluation approver |
| content owner | scenario/copy consistency | cannot turn hypothesis into product truth |
| evaluator/research owner | protocol, sessions, synthesis | cannot authorize Stage 3 |
| accessibility reviewer | prototype-layer barriers and later evidence needs | no conformance declaration |
| security reviewer | authority/privacy/simulation boundaries | no security certification |
| architecture reviewer | state/runtime claim boundaries | no stack selection by review |
| evidence custodian | version/provenance/access/retention | cannot alter observations/decisions |
| Council/approver | entry, exceptions, stop, exit and next-stage decision | cannot manufacture missing evidence |

Named individuals and conflicts of interest are required before entry. Prototype creator != evaluation approver.

## Conceptual budget ceiling

| Dimension | Proposed cap for authorization |
|---|---|
| surface families | 8 (`SF-01–08`) |
| state variants | max 24 across declared families, including 10 execution variants |
| journeys | 6, all required |
| task flows | 17 interactive + 1 represented transition |
| prototype cycles | max 2 material cycles after baseline build |
| participant sessions | 8 planning hypothesis in one round; second round requires Council condition/remaining budget |
| specialist reviews | 5: UX, accessibility, security/authority, content, architecture |
| future artifacts | 4 |
| duration | proposed maximum 10 working days excluding recruitment lead time and Council wait |
| no-progress | 2 prototype/research iterations without decision-relevant learning |

This is scope/time budgeting, not financial approval. Compensation/tool/procurement costs require separate owner/approval.

## Iteration and change policy

Baseline version `S2P-1` would freeze Council-approved PQ/HP/scenario/scope/fidelity/artifacts. Each material prototype cycle receives version, changed trace anchors, rationale, owner and expected learning. Findings are triaged as blocker, required-quality issue, desired improvement, unknown or out-of-scope. Do not change after every individual opinion; combine evidence, preserve counterexamples and prioritize blockers/high-confidence patterns. Maximum two material cycles; additional cycle requires Council amendment.

Return to Stage 1 when a finding changes C-lite actor, representative task/output, fundamental authority, required journey, requirement class or benchmark construct rather than prototype treatment.

## Invalidation rules

Reopen proposal/readiness after change to primary actor, representative task, C-lite scope/output, material authority matrix, non-compensable blockers, selected repair-shop scenario, applicable normative obligation, ARP-0007/0008 boundary, required journeys, fidelity incompatible with PQs, participant/privacy plan, or tool capability that prevents declared methods/accessibility. Record affected artifacts/evidence and comparison eligibility.

## Stage 2 entry criteria

- Council accepts Stage 1 and this proposal version;
- C-lite scope/task/output and selected scenario frozen;
- PQ-01–20, HP-01–09, Option B fidelity and SF-01–08 approved;
- future four-artifact package and budget caps approved;
- authority matrix, emergency cancel/delegation and review authority confirmed;
- Stage 2/requirement/content/research/evidence owners named;
- prototype tool capability/access review completed without selecting by this document;
- privacy/ethics, recruitment/participant, compensation, recording/storage/retention plans approved;
- specialist reviewers named and conflicts disclosed;
- evaluation protocol, blockers, stopping/invalidation/change rules approved;
- accessible participation accommodations and safety/escalation route available.

All criteria are mandatory or explicitly excepted by Council with scoped rationale/expiry. No executable gate is created.

## Stage 2 exit criteria

- four authorized artifacts complete and versioned;
- six journeys, seventeen interactive flows and required state/failure variants covered;
- approved evaluation and specialist reviews executed against declared versions;
- observations/findings linked to PQ/HP/CL/risk with limitations;
- all blockers identified; critical issues remediated and re-evaluated or escalated/accepted by proper authority;
- evidence, changes, deviations, privacy handling and unresolved unknowns recorded;
- readiness recommendation for Stage 3 proposal produced with dissent/residual risk;
- explicit statement that prototype results do not prove implementation/runtime/product quality.

Exit does not authorize Stage 3.

## Stopping rules

Stop/pause on participant/privacy/safety issue; authority conflict; critical misleading simulation; inaccessible evaluation setup; wrong/unversioned prototype; construct/scope invalidation; blocker requiring architecture/implementation to answer; tool inability to represent required path; budget/cycle cap; two no-progress iterations; evidence insufficient for decision; or Council-requested hold. Preserve partial evidence and reason.

## Stage 3 boundary

Stage 2 cannot conclude runtime accessibility/semantics/AT compatibility, security enforcement, authorization correctness, performance, browser/device compatibility, data integrity, server/client authority, real job behavior, package/library suitability, deployment/resilience, observability or production outcomes. Visual interaction in a prototype is not evidence of any of these. They require executable Stage 3 or later scoped evaluation.

## Readiness assessment

| Area | Finding |
|---|---|
| purpose/questions/traceability | ready for Council review |
| hypothesis/fidelity/scenario/scope | bounded recommendation defined |
| surfaces/flows/states/content | proposal complete; no artifact created |
| authority/long work/failure/Memory | representation contract defined; real authority confirmation pending |
| search/graph | search excluded interactively; graph deferred |
| accessibility/security/privacy | layered plan present; named reviewers/operational plans pending |
| participants/methods/ethics | bounded plan present; recruitment/consent/storage details pending |
| artifacts/tool capabilities/budget | capped; tool and financial approvals pending |
| entry/exit/invalidation/governance | explicit; role assignments and Council approval pending |

## Recommendation

**Ready with conditions** for Council to consider a separate Stage 2 creation authorization.

Conditions: accept selected repair-shop scenario, Option B neutral structured fidelity, SF-01–08, four-artifact package and budget; name roles; confirm authority/delegation; approve tool capability assessment, participant/privacy/compensation/storage plans, specialist reviewers and protocol. If any condition materially changes actor/task/scope/authority/blockers, reopen this proposal or Stage 1 as required.

This recommendation does not authorize prototype creation, recruitment, tool selection, research or implementation.

## Residual risks

Prototype mistaken for real capability/security; scenario-domain bias; neutral treatment still shaping perception; participant sample underrepresentation; inaccessible tool; staged tasks/learning effects; authority model unresolved; scope creep through states/content; self-evaluation; evidence overinterpretation; pressure to proceed to implementation from visual plausibility.
